# Recipes

This section contains "recipes" - short notebooks that provide an example of how to perform a specific action or process. Some might replicate things that already exist within the main course content; others might show something new and are made on request by students interested in certain actions. They provide minimal explanatory text, as they aim to strip away extraneous detail and provide simple examples of processing that you can edit and adapt for your own needs.
